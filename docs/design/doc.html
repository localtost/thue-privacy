01What stays on your device

To keep your place in the game, Thue stores your progress on your iPhone: solved levels, best result and time, rule drafts, the selected difficulty.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not synced through iCloud.

02Usage statistics

Thue uses Google Firebase Analytics to see which levels are hard and what to fix. See policies.google.com/privacy.
